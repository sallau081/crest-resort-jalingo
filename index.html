About Crest Resort Jalingo

Our Story, Our Commitment

A Premier Resort in Jalingo

Crest Resort Jalingo brings 5 star hospitality services to the heart of Jalingo City, Taraba State. We are more than a place to sleep: a full resort where you can stay in comfortable rooms, dine in our restaurant, unwind at the lounge bar and swimming pool, and celebrate in our event hall.

Whether you are travelling for business, relaxing with family or planning a wedding, everything you need is in one secure, welcoming place, with reliable power, strong security and a team that puts your comfort first.

Our Mission

To deliver 5 star hospitality in every part of the resort, from a warm welcome at the door to a smooth, comfortable stay, so every guest and every occasion is well cared for.

Our Vision

To be the first-choice resort in Jalingo and across Taraba State for leisure, business and celebrations.

Our Values

Comfort  |  Integrity  |  Excellence  |  Customer Focus
